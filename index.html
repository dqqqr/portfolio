01 / Backend security · Featured2026

Diet.AI secure backend.

Diet.AI creates meal plans around a user’s health conditions, so it holds sensitive data. I developed its Express microservices with security first: auth, admin, email, and support, each with its own database.

  • bcryptPasswords hashed with 12 rounds
  • JWTAccess tokens that expire after 15 minutes, with rotating refresh tokens
  • SHA-256OTPs and reset tokens stored only as hashes
  • 429Rate limits for each IP on login, registration, and password reset
Node.jsExpressPostgreSQLJWTJoi
